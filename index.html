研究主线：生成式AI赋能教育创新 Research Focus: GenAI Empowering Educational Innovation

🤖 人机协同学习 🤖 Human-AI Collaborative Learning

探索学生与双LLM智能体的交互模式，研究个人学习与协作学习中GenAI如何提升语言能力和批判性思维。通过滞后序列分析和认知网络分析，揭示不同交互模式下的认知发展规律。

Exploring interaction modes between students and dual-LLM agents, investigating how GenAI enhances language proficiency and critical thinking in individual and collaborative learning. Using lag sequential and epistemic network analysis to reveal cognitive development patterns.

GenAI × Collaboration

🎓 翻转课堂重构 🎓 Flipped Classroom Reconstruction

将生成式AI与元宇宙技术融入翻转课堂，构建沉浸式学习环境。通过实证研究验证技术增强型翻转课堂对学习成效的影响，为未来教育模式提供实践路径。

Integrating GenAI and Metaverse technologies into flipped classrooms to create immersive learning environments. Empirically validating the impact of technology-enhanced flipped classrooms on learning outcomes.

Metaverse × Flipped Classroom

💡 创造力培养 💡 Creativity Development

基于PISA 2022数据，运用模糊集定性比较分析（fsQCA）探究ICT使用如何影响学生创造性思维。识别多种配置路径，为数字时代创造力培养提供循证依据。

Using fuzzy-set Qualitative Comparative Analysis (fsQCA) on PISA 2022 data to investigate how ICT use influences students' creative thinking. Identifying multiple configurational pathways for creativity development.

ICT × Creativity

🌐 教育元宇宙 🌐 Educational Metaverse

从知识共享到知识共创，研究去中心化知识观在教育元宇宙中的实现。探索虚拟教学共同体如何促进教育公平，为未来学习空间设计提供理论框架。

From knowledge sharing to knowledge co-creation, studying the implementation of decentralized knowledge views in educational metaverse. Exploring how virtual teaching communities promote educational equity.

Educational Metaverse

代表性研究项目 Featured Research Projects

双LLM智能体交互研究 Dual-LLM Agent Interaction Study

设计人性化双LLM GenAI智能体，探索个人与协作交互模式对语言学习和批判性思维的差异化影响。揭示了协作模式促进语言能力，而个人模式强化批判思维意识的独特机制。

Designed a humanized dual-LLM GenAI agent, exploring differential impacts of individual and collaborative interaction modes on language learning and critical thinking. Revealed unique mechanisms where collaborative mode enhances language proficiency while individual mode strengthens critical thinking awareness.

Interactive Learning Environments (2025) SSCI Q1
🔄

GenAI+元宇宙翻转课堂 GenAI+Metaverse Flipped Classroom

创新性地将生成式AI与元宇宙技术整合到翻转课堂模型中，通过滞后序列分析和认知网络分析验证学习效果。为技术增强型教学设计提供实证支持。

Innovatively integrated GenAI and Metaverse technologies into the flipped classroom model. Validated learning outcomes through lag sequential and epistemic network analysis, providing empirical support for technology-enhanced instructional design.

Educational Technology Research and Development (2025) SSCI Q1
💡

ICT与创造性思维配置研究 ICT & Creative Thinking Configuration Study

基于PISA 2022大规模数据，运用fsQCA方法识别促进学生创造性思维的ICT使用配置路径。研究发现，多种组合策略均可达成创造力培养目标。

Based on PISA 2022 large-scale data, used fsQCA method to identify ICT usage configuration pathways that promote students' creative thinking. Found that multiple combination strategies can achieve creativity development goals.

International Journal of Educational Research (2025) SSCI Q1
🏆

教育中的AI综述研究 AI in Education Review

系统综述2010-2020年间人工智能在教育领域的应用研究。该文被认定为ESI高被引论文，引用量超过1200次，为教育AI研究提供了重要参考框架。

Systematic review of AI applications in education from 2010-2020. Recognized as an ESI Highly Cited Paper with over 1200 citations, providing an important reference framework for educational AI research.
